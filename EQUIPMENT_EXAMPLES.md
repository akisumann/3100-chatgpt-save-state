# Equipment Design Examples

These are calibration examples for the equipment system. They do **not** import the source character or any unrelated setting into the 3100 world canon.

## 氷室 calibration example: 《アイススパイクアーマー》

**Lv50-equivalent major equipment**

An add-on spike assembly mounted over an armadillo-like shell. It is intended to enhance a spinning charge rather than function like a conventional weapon or a new full suit of armor.

### Raw stat bonus

- ATK **+42**
- DEF **+28**
- SPD **+21**
- Total raw numerical bonus: **+91**

This is intentionally much lower than the roughly +238 full raw-stat potential of a Lv50 major equipment slot. Most of the item's value is spent on its specialized behavior.

### Special properties

- **Spiked charge impact:** the spikes improve the bite and damage of spinning collisions, but they are not treated as having the cutting performance of a proper sword or dedicated blade.
- **Ground bite / turning control:** the spikes can catch and bite into the ground, improving traction, turning, braking, and control while spinning.
- **Ice-film reinforcement:** the attachment modestly reinforces the user's existing ice defensive membrane and helps it remain stable during high-impact movement.

### Design rationale

The physical structure does not fundamentally make the user's base shell much tougher, so its raw DEF increase should remain modest. Likewise, exposed spikes can add impact and penetration to a spinning charge, but should not receive sword-like offensive values simply because they are sharp.

This example is a benchmark for the broader rule that highly specialized equipment can have relatively low raw stat bonuses while spending most of its level-equivalent performance budget on movement, control, interaction with an existing ability, or other special functionality.
