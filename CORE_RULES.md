# Core Rules

## World scale

- Levels run from Lv1 to Lv100.
- Lv1–10: ordinary people to beginners.
- Lv11–25: competent / established.
- Lv26–40: experienced.
- Lv41–60: upper-tier.
- Lv61–80: heroic.
- Lv81–99: legendary.
- Lv100: top tier.
- Roughly within 5 levels is comparable; 10 levels is a clear advantage; 20 is usually overwhelming; 30+ is normally not a meaningful contest.
- Equipment, compatibility, skills and unusual abilities can create exceptions.

## Status grades

- F: very poor / major weakness
- E: weak
- D: ordinary-person level
- C: profession-wide median / standard
- B: clear strength
- A: very strong
- S: exceptional strength

C is not average for the character's exact level. It is the median across the profession in the setting.

## Aki

- Name: アキ
- Adult male
- Class: Summoner
- Level: 50
- Adventurer
- Normally does not fight directly.
- Main combat value is roster breadth, judgment, summon/return timing, and rapid substitutions.
- The user controls Aki's choices. Do not over-determine his dialogue, emotions, or personality.

## Summoning

- Aki mainly summons adult women from other worlds.
- Usual targets are Lv50 or below.
- Combatants and noncombat specialists can both be summoned.
- Maximum simultaneous summons: 4.
- Maximum active party size including Aki: 5.
- Registration count is not limited to four.
- Summoning and returning are quick enough for frequent combat swaps.
- Returned summons go back to their original worlds.
- Returning does not itself fully heal injuries.
- Once registered, a summon can be called again later.

## Base and lifestyle

- Aki is based in a royal-capital-scale city with standard fantasy metropolitan and guild infrastructure.
- He owns a spacious house suitable for several people.
- His finances are comfortable enough that ordinary living costs and routine adventure preparation are rarely constraints.

## Design policy

- Keep worldbuilding comparatively compact.
- Put greater detail into characters.
- Add world details when relevant rather than filling everything in advance.
- Do not silently rewrite established characters or rules for scene convenience.
- Do not invent an unprovided summon merely because it would be convenient.
