# Core Rules — Fantasy Summoner

These are stable project facts and operating constraints. Do not change them for convenience.

## World scale

- Fantasy world with character levels from Lv1 to Lv100.
- Broad strength bands:
  - Lv1–10: ordinary people to beginners
  - Lv11–25: competent / established
  - Lv26–40: experienced
  - Lv41–60: upper-tier
  - Lv61–80: heroic
  - Lv81–99: legendary
  - Lv100: top tier
- Rough combat relationship:
  - within about 5 levels: comparable
  - about 10 levels: clear advantage
  - about 20 levels: usually overwhelming
  - 30+ levels: normally not a meaningful contest
- Equipment, compatibility, skills and unusual abilities can create exceptions.

## Status grades

- F: very poor / major weakness
- E: weak
- D: ordinary-person level
- C: occupational median / standard across the full profession
- B: clear strength
- A: very strong
- S: exceptional strength

C is not "average for this exact level"; it is the profession-wide median across the setting. A Lv50 specialist will therefore often have B–S in core strengths.

## Aki

- Name: アキ
- Adult male
- Class: Summoner, a mage-derived profession
- Level: 50
- Adventurer
- Normally does not fight directly.
- Main combat value is roster breadth, judgment, summon/return timing, and rapid substitutions.
- User controls Aki's decisions. Do not over-determine his dialogue, feelings, choices, or personality when the user has not done so.

## Summoning

- Aki mainly summons adult women from other worlds.
- His usual targets are Lv50 or below.
- Summons may be combatants or noncombat specialists such as merchants, craftsmen, healers or alchemists.
- Maximum simultaneous summons: 4.
- Maximum active party size including Aki: 5.
- Registration count is not limited to four.
- Summoning and returning are quick enough for frequent combat swaps.
- A returned summon goes back to her original world.
- Returning does not itself fully heal injuries.
- Once registered, a summon can be called again later.

## Base and lifestyle

- Aki's base is a large royal-capital-scale city with normal fantasy metropolitan services and guild infrastructure.
- He owns a spacious house suitable for several people.
- His finances are comfortable enough that ordinary living costs and routine adventure preparation are rarely constraints.

## Setting style

- Keep world setting comparatively compact.
- Put greater detail into characters.
- Add world details when they become relevant rather than filling the map in advance.
- Existing setting and existing characters must not be silently rewritten to suit a scene.
- Do not create an unprovided summon or alter an established summon merely because it would be convenient.
